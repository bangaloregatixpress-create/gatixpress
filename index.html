Help Center

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know before booking your move.

How much does a house shifting service cost?

Pricing depends on distance, home size, number of items, packing requirements, floor level and selected vehicle. Get an instant quote for an estimated price.

Do you provide packing and unpacking?

Yes. Professional packing, loading, transportation, unloading and unpacking options can be selected according to your relocation requirements.

Can I track my shipment?

Yes. Use your booking ID in the shipment tracking section to check the latest movement and delivery status.

Do you move vehicles between cities?

Vehicle transportation can be requested for cars and two-wheelers, subject to route and service availability.

How early should I book my move?

Booking a few days in advance is recommended, especially during weekends, month-end and peak relocation periods.
